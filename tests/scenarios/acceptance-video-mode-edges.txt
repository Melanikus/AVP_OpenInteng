# "loading N%" while it read; (6) no press without a video: with no video the screen offers NO control, and Cancel only while one is read or made (so the engine's
# own "Drop a video first" and "Nothing is being read or made" guards can't be reached from the screen); (7) a number typed past the game's 1 to 450 (451, and 0):
# refused in words, nothing written; (8) a second drop replaces the first: loadedclips 2, its fit, cut and volume start again.
