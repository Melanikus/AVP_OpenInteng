# inteng's editor is NOT two clicks for corners: it draws the volume as a RED WIREFRAME CUBE (FUN_00583130 with 0xff0000), writes six help lines at (16, 0) ("S : Set volume to module extents", "X : Expand volume to include
# module", "Shift+Left Click : Select face of cube", "+/- : Move selected face", "Hold shift to move face faster", "Press return when finished"), a left click picks a MODULE (red), Shift + left click picks a FACE of the
# cube (green), S sets the cube to the module's box, X grows it to include the box, + / - move the selected face (2 m a second, 20 with Shift), Return or the right button ends and the numbers are written into the strategy.
# The manual (qa.txt, "death volumes"): "Press V to edit the volume ... Left Click: Selects a module. S: ... X: ... Shift + Left Click (on the volume cube): Selects a face on the cube. The face is shown as green. +/- : Moves
# the selected face in or out. Right click, or press enter when finished."
